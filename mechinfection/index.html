MechInfection

Survival Horror Co-op Campaign PvE Crafting Atmospheric 2-4 Player Co-op Windows

Help came too late. The roads are empty, the cities silent, and those who stayed stopped being human long ago. Four survivors are stranded at the edge of this world with one goal between them: hold out long enough to get away.

By day, scavenge supplies across shifting, procedurally-changing locations. By night, defend your shelter from the things outside. Craft weapons, set traps, cultivate gardens, and build relationships with NPCs to survive and eventually escape.

  1. 01

    2-4 Player Co-op

    Survive together or not at all. Built from the ground up for small squads.

  2. 02

    Day / Night Loop

    Scavenge in daylight, barricade and defend when the sun goes down.

  3. 03

    Crafting & Traps

    Build weapons and defenses from whatever the ruins leave behind.

  4. 04

    Gardening

    Cultivate what food you can in a world that doesn't want you to.

  5. 05

    NPC Relationships

    Not everything outside is an enemy. Trust is earned, and it matters.

  6. 06

    Shifting Locations

    Procedurally-changing environments keep every run unfamiliar.
